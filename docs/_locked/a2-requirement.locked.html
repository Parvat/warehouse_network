Five questions, about two minutes. No jargon, and nothing here needs an account — you will end up with a rack type, the reason for it, and dealers who supply it.

1

How many different products do you store?

Distinct SKUs, not total pallets.

2

How many pallets of the same product?

3

Dated food, pharmaceuticals or lot-traced parts usually do.

4

How much moves in and out each day?

Heavy traffic needs pick faces, which works against dense storage.

5

What forklifts do you have?
