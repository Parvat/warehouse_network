Notes

  1. Anonymous users get everything about the market. Signed-in users get to enter it.

Buy is weeks. Build is months. Own is decades — and it is the only phase that repeats.

Notes

  1. Warehouse is the root entity, project is an episode. Making Project the root orphans the customer at completion — exactly where the recurring revenue lives.
  2. Annual inspection reminders are the retention mechanic — the only thing that reliably brings a customer back.
  3. Expansion starts from the existing layout, not a blank page. Enormous repeat-purchase advantage.
  4. Mini-WMS stays shallow: occupied/empty, SKU, pallet ID, find, move. Add receiving and waves and you are competing with Manhattan, and losing.
A customer who buys racking and leaves with a PDF is a one-time transaction. A customer who ends up with a structured layout is yours for twenty years. That — not the purchase — is the real conversion event of this business.
